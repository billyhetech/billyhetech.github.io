一句话结论：轻轻不收集你的任何数据。App 没有服务器、没有账号体系，所有照片分析都在你的 iPhone 本地完成。唯一会产生网络流量的情形，是把你自己存在 iCloud 里的照片下载回本机以便分析，这一步由 Apple 的系统框架完成，照片不会流向我们或任何第三方。In one line: Feather collects nothing. No photo reaches us or any third party.

一、我们收集哪些信息1. What we collect

我们不收集任何个人信息，也不收集任何设备信息或使用数据。具体而言：We collect no personal information, no device information, and no usage data. Specifically:

  • 不收集、不上传、不存储你的照片、视频或其中的任何内容。We do not collect, upload, or store your photos, videos, or anything derived from them.
  • 不收集姓名、邮箱、手机号、身份证件等身份信息（App 无需注册或登录）。We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any identity document — the app requires no sign-up and no sign-in.
  • 不收集设备标识符（IDFA / IDFV）、位置、通讯录、日历等信息。We do not collect device identifiers (IDFA / IDFV), location, contacts, or calendar data.
  • 不使用任何第三方分析、广告、崩溃上报或推送 SDK。We use no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or push-notification SDKs.

关于 iCloud 照片与流量About iCloud photos and data usage

如果你开启了 iCloud「优化 iPhone 存储空间」，部分照片在本机只保留低清占位图。为了让这些照片也能参与相似度分析、能被正常预览，轻轻会通过 Apple 的 PhotoKit 向 iCloud 请求下载展示所需的照片数据。关于这件事，有三点需要说清楚：If you use iCloud "Optimize iPhone Storage", some photos exist on your device only as low-resolution placeholders. So that those photos can still be previewed and compared, Feather asks Apple’s PhotoKit to fetch the image data it needs from iCloud. Three things about that are worth stating plainly:

  • 这是下载，不是上传。数据方向是「你的 iCloud → 你的 iPhone」，我们没有服务器，照片不会、也无法流向我们或任何第三方。This is a download, not an upload. The direction is your iCloud → your iPhone. We have no server; your photos cannot reach us or any third party.
  • 扫描只取降采样缩略图（相似度分析用 256×256）。只有当你正在查看一张动图、并且它仅存于 iCloud 时，系统才会下载该动图的完整数据以便播放；不会批量下载。Scanning fetches downsampled thumbnails only (256×256 for similarity analysis). Only when you are actively viewing an animated image that exists solely in iCloud does the system fetch that one image’s full data for playback. Nothing is bulk-downloaded.
  • 过程可见。扫描界面会实时显示「其中 N 张从 iCloud 下载 · 只下载，不上传」，单张照片加载时也会标明来源，你随时可以知道流量花在哪里。The process is visible. The scan screen shows a live count of photos fetched from iCloud, labelled "download only, never upload", and individual photos are labelled while they load — you can always see where the traffic went.

这一行为使用的是系统相册与 iCloud 之间的既有通道，与你在系统「照片」App 里浏览一张 iCloud 照片时发生的事情完全相同。This uses the existing channel between the system photo library and iCloud — exactly what happens when you scroll past an iCloud photo in Apple’s own Photos app.

关于照片详情里的拍摄位置About capture location in photo details

「照片详情」会显示这张照片自带的拍摄坐标（如果有）。这个坐标只从你本机的照片读出来、只显示给你看，不会被上传，也不会被拿去查询地名——反向地理编码需要联网请求，我们刻意没有做，宁可只显示一行原始经纬度。The photo details screen shows the capture coordinates embedded in a photo, when it has any. Those coordinates are read from the photo on your device and displayed only to you. They are never uploaded, and they are never turned into a place name: reverse geocoding would require a network request, so we deliberately do not do it and show the raw latitude and longitude instead.

三、本地存储了什么3. What is stored on your device

轻轻只在你的设备本地保存以下与照片内容无关的记录，用于展示整理历史与统计：Feather keeps only the following records — none of which contain image content — on your device, so it can show your cleanup history and statistics:

  • 每次整理的时间、处理张数、删除张数、释放空间估算值、归档张数与目标相册名。For each session: the time, how many photos were reviewed, how many were deleted, an estimate of the space freed, how many were filed, and the destination album names.
  • 被删除照片的系统标识符（localIdentifier，一串不含任何图像内容的字符串）。The system identifier of each deleted photo (localIdentifier — a string that contains no image content).
  • 免费功能的使用计数（用于判断是否已用完免费额度）。A usage counter for the free allowance, used to tell whether the daily quota is spent.

这些记录随 App 一同保存在设备上，卸载 App 即被系统删除，我们无法访问。These records live on your device alongside the app. Uninstalling it removes them, and we have no way to access them at any point.

七、你的权利7. Your rights

依据《中华人民共和国个人信息保护法》及其他适用法律，你享有查阅、复制、更正、删除个人信息等权利。由于本 App 不收集任何个人信息，这些权利在实践中体现为：Under the Personal Information Protection Law of the People’s Republic of China — and under comparable laws such as the GDPR where they apply to you — you have rights to access, copy, correct, and delete your personal information. Because this app collects none, those rights take the following practical form:

  • 撤回授权：随时在「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片」中撤回或调整相册访问权限。Withdraw access: revoke or narrow photo library access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.
  • 删除本地记录：卸载 App 即可删除全部本地整理记录。Delete local records: uninstalling the app removes every cleanup record it kept.
  • 恢复误删照片：在系统「照片 → 最近删除」中恢复，删除后 30 天内有效。Recover deleted photos: restore them from Photos → Recently Deleted, for 30 days after deletion.
