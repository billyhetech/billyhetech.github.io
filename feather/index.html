核心功能

  • 按来源挑照片回顾：盲盒随机、去年今日、按月、按类型（截屏 / 实况 / 自拍 / 人像等）、按相簿。
  • 整理即归档：边整理边把保留的照片归入相册，不用再回照片库二次操作。
  • 相似照片分组对比：连拍与重复照片自动聚成一组，一次挑出最好的那张。
  • 可信删除：删除时实时显示已释放空间，照片进入系统「最近删除」，30 天内可恢复。

隐私承诺

轻轻没有服务器，没有账号体系，没有任何分析或广告 SDK。所有照片分析（包括相似度比对） 都使用 iOS 系统框架在设备本地完成，不会收集你的任何数据，也不会把你的照片传给任何人。 唯一的网络行为，是把你自己存在 iCloud 里的照片下载回本机以便分析—— 方向是「你的 iCloud → 你的设备」，由 Apple 系统框架完成。

App Store 隐私标签：不收集数据（Data Not Collected）。

Feather (English)

Feather is an on-device photo cleaner for iPhone. Swipe to keep or delete, compare near-duplicate bursts side by side, and file the keepers into an album as you go.

  • Everything stays on your iPhone. No server, no account, no analytics or ad SDKs. All analysis — including similarity matching — runs through Apple's on-device frameworks. App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.
  • The one kind of network traffic, explained. If you use iCloud "Optimize iPhone Storage", Feather asks Apple's PhotoKit to fetch thumbnails of your own photos back from your own iCloud so they can be analyzed. The direction is iCloud → your device. Nothing is ever uploaded to us; we have no server to upload it to.
  • Deletions are reversible. Photos move to the system "Recently Deleted" album and can be restored within 30 days. Freed space is shown as you go.
  • One-time purchase. Not a subscription — no recurring charges, no ads.
