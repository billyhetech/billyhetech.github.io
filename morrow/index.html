慢慢能做什么 What it does

机器发现变化，家长赋予意义，慢慢完成故事Morrow finds the change. You give it meaning. Together, a story.

孩子的照片越拍越多，却很少再被翻看。慢慢从相册中找出值得留下的变化，再由你讲述照片背后的故事。Photos of your child pile up and are rarely revisited. Morrow finds the changes worth keeping, and you tell the story behind them.

发现成长中的变化Discover how they've grown

同一地点相隔数年的「那时 / 现在」，从扶着站到独立行走的成长路径，都能从照片中自动发现；只呈现照片能证明的事实。Then-and-now pairs from the same place years apart, and the path from pulling up to stand to walking. Morrow finds them in your photos and shows only what the photos can prove.

讲述与成稿From your words to a draft

为每张照片说几句或写几句，语音在本机转为文字并整理为初稿；故事中只保留你确认过的内容。Speak or type a few lines for each photo. Speech is transcribed on device and shaped into a draft, and only what you confirm is kept.

时光与周岁回忆Timeline and First Birthday Recap

每天的照片整理为时光中的成长瞬间；孩子满一岁时，从十二个月中各选一张，生成十二宫格与回忆视频。Each day's photos become Moments in your Timeline. At one year, one photo from each month becomes a 12-photo grid and a Memory Video.

分享卡、回忆视频与 PDFShare cards, Memory Videos and PDFs

完成的故事可发给家人、存到相册，或导出排版好的 PDF 成长书。Send a finished story to family, save it to Photos, or export a typeset PDF growth book.

隐私承诺 Privacy

孩子的照片，只留在你的 iPhone 上Your child's photos stay on your iPhone

慢慢没有服务器与账号体系，也不含任何统计或广告 SDK。识别孩子、挑选照片与语音转写均在本机完成。Morrow has no server, no accounts, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. Recognizing your child, choosing photos and transcribing speech all happen on your iPhone.

唯一对外发送的信息，是为显示地名提交给 Apple 地图的一处大致坐标，不含照片及其他信息，可随时在 App 中关闭。The only information sent off your iPhone is one approximate location, sent to Apple Maps to show a place name. It includes no photos or other data, and you can turn it off anytime in the app.
