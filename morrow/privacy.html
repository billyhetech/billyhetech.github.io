隐私政策Privacy Policy

一、慢慢不收集你的数据1. Morrow Doesn't Collect Your Data

慢慢没有账号、没有服务器，也不使用任何统计、广告或第三方追踪工具。你的照片、孩子的资料、故事、录音和文字都只保存在这台 iPhone 上，开发者看不到，也无法读取。Morrow has no accounts, no servers, and no analytics, ads or third-party tracking. Your photos, your child's details, stories, recordings and words stay only on this iPhone; the developer can't see or read them.

二、个人信息处理者2. Data Controller

慢慢由高园（个体工商户「北京贝贝之星孕婴服装专营店」经营者）开发和运营，是本政策所称的个人信息处理者。联系邮箱：graceglabs@163.com。Morrow is developed and operated by Gao Yuan, sole proprietor of Beijing Beibeizhixing Yunying Fuzhuang Zhuanyingdian (an individually-owned business registered in China), the data controller under this policy. Contact: graceglabs@163.com.

三、照片3. Photo

在你允许后，慢慢通过系统的照片权限读取相册。认出孩子、挑选照片、发现成长变化所需的画面与面部特征都在这台 iPhone 上计算，只保存在本机，不会上传，也不纳入 iCloud 备份。With your permission, Morrow reads your photo library through the system's photo access. The image and face features used to recognize your child, pick photos and find changes are computed on this iPhone and stored only here—never uploaded and not included in iCloud Backup.

慢慢不会改动或删除相册中的照片。只有在你轻点「存到相册」时，才会把那一张分享卡、宫格图或那一段回忆视频存入相册。Morrow never changes or deletes photos in your library. It only adds that one share card, grid image or Memory Video when you tap Save to Photos.

四、敏感个人信息4. Sensitive Personal Information

为在照片中识别孩子，慢慢会在本机计算人脸特征。人脸特征与不满十四周岁儿童的个人信息属于敏感个人信息，仅在本机处理和保存，仅用于识别孩子的照片，不上传、不共享，开发者无法获取。清除本地分析数据或删除 App 时一并删除。To recognize your child in photos, Morrow computes face features on this iPhone. Face features and the personal information of children under 14 are sensitive personal information. They are processed and stored only on this device, used only to identify your child's photos, never uploaded or shared, and inaccessible to the developer. They are deleted when you clear local analysis data or delete the app.

五、孩子的信息5. Your Child's Information

慢慢面向家长（监护人），不面向儿童。孩子的名字、生日与头像照片由你填写或选择，仅保存在本机，用于整理照片和计算月龄。Morrow is for parents and guardians, not children. Your child's name, birthday and profile photo are provided by you, stored only on this iPhone, and used to organize photos and calculate age.

六、录音与语音转写6. Recordings & Transcription

在你允许后，慢慢使用麦克风录下你的回答。语音转文字只在这台 iPhone 上进行，从不使用网络识别。录音保存在本机，不会上传，也不纳入 iCloud 备份。With your permission, Morrow uses the microphone to record your answers. Speech is transcribed only on this iPhone, never with online recognition. Recordings stay on this device; they're never uploaded and aren't included in iCloud Backup.

七、系统权限7. Permissions

照片：读取相册，用于整理照片与发现故事。添加到照片：仅在你轻点「存到相册」时写入该文件。麦克风：录制你的讲述。语音识别：在本机将录音转为文字。Photos: to read your library, organize photos and find stories. Add to Photos: only to save a file when you tap Save to Photos. Microphone: to record what you say. Speech Recognition: to transcribe recordings on this iPhone.

各项权限在首次使用时由系统询问；拒绝仅影响对应功能，可随时在 iPhone「设置 → 慢慢」中更改。iPhone asks for each permission the first time it's needed. Declining affects only that feature, and you can change it anytime in iPhone Settings → Morrow.

八、地名8. Place Names

为故事、成长瞬间和照片详情显示地名时，慢慢通过 iOS 地理编码服务，将一处地点的大致坐标（精度约 11 米，不含照片及其他信息）发送至 Apple 地图（接收方：Apple，依其隐私政策处理），仅用于获取地名。这是慢慢唯一对外发送的信息。地名仅保存在本机，可随时在「我的 → 隐私与安全」中关闭查询。To show place names for stories, Daily Moments and photo details, Morrow uses iOS geocoding to send one approximate location (about 11 m precision, with no photo or other information) to Apple Maps, operated by Apple under its privacy policy, solely to retrieve the place name. This is the only information Morrow sends off the device. Place names are stored only on this iPhone. You can turn off lookups anytime in Me → Privacy & Security.

九、文字润色9. Polishing

支持 Apple 智能的 iPhone 上，润色建议由本机模型生成，你的话不会上传。不支持的设备不提供润色。On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, polishing suggestions come from the on-device model and your words are never uploaded. Other devices don't offer polishing.

十、购买10. Purchases

慢慢 Pro 与单篇故事的购买由 Apple 处理，慢慢看不到你的付款信息。慢慢在本机向 App Store 查询购买状态，并在本机（包括钥匙串）记录哪些故事、视频已经解锁。Purchases of Morrow Pro and single stories are handled by Apple; Morrow never sees your payment details. Morrow checks your purchase status with the App Store on this device and records on this device (including the keychain) which stories and videos are unlocked.

十一、分享11. Share

你发给家人的分享卡、PDF 和回忆视频，通过系统分享面板发送到你选择的地方，慢慢不知道、也不记录发给了谁。Share cards, PDFs and Memory Videos you send go through the system share sheet to wherever you choose. Morrow doesn't know or record who receives them.

十二、删除12. Delete

你可以在「我的 → 隐私与安全」中清除本地分析数据，或在孩子资料中删除孩子。删除慢慢 App 会删除它保存的全部内容，相册中的照片不受影响。You can clear local analysis data in Me → Privacy & Security, or delete a child from their profile. Deleting the Morrow app deletes everything it stores; the photos in your library aren't affected.

慢慢的内容保存在本机，直至你将其删除或删除 App。Morrow's content stays on this iPhone until you delete it or the app.

十三、你的权利13. Your Rights

你可以在慢慢中查看、修改和删除孩子的资料与故事，在「我的 → 数据与备份」中导出故事，在「隐私与安全」中关闭地名查询、清除本地分析数据，并在 iPhone 设置中撤回权限。如需帮助，请发送邮件，我们将在 15 个工作日内回复。In Morrow, you can view, edit and delete your child's details and stories, export stories in Me → Data & Backup, turn off place lookups and clear local analysis data in Privacy & Security, and withdraw permissions in iPhone Settings. If you need help, email us and we'll reply within 15 business days.

十四、变更与联系14. Changes & Contact

本政策变更时，会在 App 内更新并标明日期。如有疑问，请发送邮件至 graceglabs@163.com。If this policy changes, it will be updated and dated in the app. Questions? Email graceglabs@163.com.
